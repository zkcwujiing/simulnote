# V6 评测语料 · 一次典型的英文业务例会（约 10 分钟口播量）
#
# 为什么要有这个文件：docs/06 §6.2 说 V6 要判断「TextRank 能不能挑出真正重要的句子」。
# 光看输出靠人感觉，结论就没法比对、也没法回归。所以这里把语料**先标好**。
#
# 格式（2026/10/7 重做过一次，原因见下）：
#
#   "@ " 开头的行 = 这句话是**要点**（既是 ground truth，也是要喂给引擎的讲话）
#   "- " 开头的行 = 这句话是铺垫 / 过程 / 闲聊，不该进要点列表（同样要喂给引擎）
#   "#" 开头的行  = 注释，不参与计算
#
# 评测脚本 scripts/summary-bench.mjs 会跑两种喂法：
#   speech —— 把 @ 与 - 两类行**都**喂给引擎（诚实档：真实场景就是这样，没人提前标好）
#   full   —— **只喂 @ 行**（对照组：要点的字面已经在输入里，只看匹配算法坏没坏）
# speech 是更诚实的那个数字，报告里排在前面。
#
# ⚠️ 为什么在 2026/10/7 重做
#    旧版把「人工标注」与「讲话原文」分成两类行，而标注是**改写**而不是逐字摘录，
#    有些标注甚至比原文更聪明 —— 标注写 `@ The board rejected the Northwind
#    acquisition…`，对应的原句只说了「62 要 95，董事会觉得站不住」；`churn 2.1→3.4`
#    这个数字**原文里根本没有**。后果：speech 档的 recall 天然偏低，而且没人分得清
#    哪部分是引擎的错。新版改成**一句话一行、逐句标「是不是要点」**，并把原先只
#    存在于标注里的那几条事实**补回讲话原文**（churn 数字、推迟欧洲发布、Sarah 的
#    交付日期）—— 不然引擎永远没机会挑到它们，那不是评测，是陷阱。
#
# ⚠️ 诚实声明：标注是一个人做的，是个**弱标准**。它不能证明「摘要好」，
#    但足以回答「引擎是不是在乱挑」以及「改动之后有没有变差」。
#    真正上线前仍然要让真人读一遍输出（探针页的 V6 那一节就是干这个的）。

- Good morning everyone, thanks for joining on short notice.
- Let me walk through where we are on the quarter and then we have three decisions to make.
@ First, the top line: revenue came in at 4.8 million dollars, which is up 23 percent from last quarter.
@ Gross margin held steady at 61 percent even though infrastructure cost went up.
- The reason we did not lose margin is that we renegotiated the cloud contract in February.
@ Enterprise renewals carried the quarter, especially in the manufacturing segment.
@ We lost two logos in retail, and honestly both of them were price decisions rather than product problems.
- On the product side, the new billing pipeline finally shipped.
@ It shipped three weeks late, and I want to be direct about why.
@ The migration to PostgreSQL took longer than estimated because we discovered data quality problems halfway through.
@ The good news is that latency dropped from 820 milliseconds to 140 milliseconds after the rewrite.
@ Support ticket volume is down about 18 percent since that change.
- Now the harder part, which is the incident we had on the ninth.
@ It was 41 minutes of degraded service, and about a third of our customers saw errors.
@ The root cause was that we pushed a configuration change without a staged rollout.
@ Nobody was paged for eleven minutes because the alert threshold was set for the old traffic pattern.
- I am not going to blame anyone individually, but we do need to change the process.
@ We decided that no configuration change may go out without a staged rollout to ten percent of traffic first.
@ We also decided to move the alert threshold review into the weekly operations meeting.
- Let me move to the plan for next quarter.
@ The headline is that we want to double the size of the data team.
- That means four hires, and realistically two of them will take a quarter to become productive.
@ Hiring is the bottleneck, not budget.
@ On the European launch, we are going to postpone it to the second half of the year.
- I know that launch has been on the roadmap for a while.
- The reason is that the localization work is bigger than we scoped, and we do not want to launch half ready.
@ Sarah put together a revised estimate and it came out at roughly eleven weeks of engineering.
- There was also the acquisition question, and I should report what happened.
@ The board rejected the Northwind acquisition because the valuation was too high.
@ We offered 62 million dollars and they wanted 95 million dollars.
- I personally think walking away was the right call, but reasonable people disagree.
- Two more things before we close.
@ Churn moved from 2.1 percent to 3.4 percent this quarter and we do not fully understand that movement yet.
- I do not want to guess about it in this meeting.
@ Sarah will send the revised forecast by Friday the fifteenth of March.
@ The platform team will publish a written incident review before the next meeting.
- Also please read the incident review before the meeting, not during it.
- There is one more risk worth naming out loud.
@ There is a real risk that our main vendor will raise prices again next year.
- Their contract has a price review clause and we should get ahead of it.
- Okay, that is everything I had. Thanks again, and let us keep this to forty five minutes next time.
